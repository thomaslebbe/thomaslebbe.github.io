I am a macroeconomist fascinated by one question: *how can economies decarbonize without undermining their public finances?* I pursue this question as a PhD researcher at Ghent University, where I held a FWO doctoral fellowship (2021–2025); my PhD defence is expected in 2027. Before the PhD I studied economics at Ghent University and UCLouvain.

## Research Interests

- **Energy transition and climate policy** — how carbon pricing, green subsidies and public investment shape the shift to clean energy
- **Fiscal policy and public debt sustainability** — financing the transition when debt is high and populations are ageing
- **Structural macroeconomic modelling** — DSGE, OLG and HANK models built for policy analysis
- **Panel econometrics**

Read more about my work on the [Research page](research.html).
