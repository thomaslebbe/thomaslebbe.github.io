I am a macroeconomist fascinated by one question: *how can economies decarbonize without worsening inequality and public finances?* I pursue this question as a PhD researcher at Ghent University, where I held a FWO doctoral fellowship (2021–2025); my PhD defence is expected in 2027. Before the PhD I studied economics at Ghent University and UCLouvain.

## Research Interests

- **Energy transition and climate policy** — how carbon pricing, green subsidies and public investment shape the shift to clean energy.
- **Technology and policy** — how fiscal policy and innovation intertwine
- **Fiscal policy and public debt sustainability** — financing the clean energy transition when debt is high and population is ageing
- **Structural macroeconomic modelling** — DSGE, OLG and HANK models built for policy analysis
- **Panel econometrics**
