The energy transition is often studied as a technological problem. It is
just as much a fiscal one: carbon taxes, green subsidies and public
investment all run through government budgets, and these budgets are
already under pressure from debt and ageing. My research asks how
economies can decarbonize without undermining their public finances.

I approach this with structural general equilibrium models (DSGE and OLG)
of climate policy and the energy transition, combined with panel
econometric work on energy prices and the energy mix. This keeps the
models honest about the data.
