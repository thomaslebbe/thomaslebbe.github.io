About

I am a PhD researcher in macroeconomics at Ghent University, where I held a competitive FWO doctoral fellowship (2021–2025). My research develops policy-relevant general equilibrium models — DSGE and OLG — with a focus on climate policy, the energy transition, and fiscal sustainability, combined with panel-econometric work on energy prices and energy-mix dynamics.

I hold a Research Master's in Economics from UCLouvain (cum laude, 2021) and an MSc in Economics from Ghent University (magna cum laude, 2019). My PhD defence is expected in 2027.

Working Papers

The Energy Transition in Long-Run Macroeconomics
Working paper, 2026
A structural general-equilibrium model of the long-run energy transition, estimated on historical data, with counterfactual decompositions quantifying the contribution of individual policy channels.
The Elasticity of Substitution in Energy
with Freddy Heylen — working paper, 2026
Empirical estimation of energy–capital–labour substitution using newly constructed long-run price series.
Heat Distributed Unequally in the Sovereign Bond Market
with Azzam Santosa — working paper, 2026
An empirical analysis of how heat shocks are priced in sovereign bond markets.
Green Fiscal Policy in a High-Debt, Ageing Society
Work in progress
An OLG analysis of the energy transition under fiscal and demographic constraints.
